# economic-history-dataset
📄 Description

This repository provides a structured chronological dataset covering the evolution of economic thought, societal development, historical events, and monetary systems from approximately 9500 BC to the present day.

The dataset integrates multiple dimensions of human history, including key figures such as economists, prophets, and Sahaba, along with their names, nationalities, associated works, and schools of thought. It aligns these contributors with the historical contexts in which they lived, including major crises, societal transformations, and changes in monetary systems.

Each entry is organized to highlight the relationship between intellectual contributions and the environments that shaped them. The dataset emphasizes the idea that economic and philosophical thought is deeply influenced by historical conditions, and that shifts in society and crises often drive innovation in both ideas and financial systems.

📊 Dataset Structure Includes:
Date in detail
Name and nationality of economists / prophets / Sahaba
Book titles or reference links
Schools of thought
Crisis or historical events
Evolution of money systems
Societal context
General dates (BC/AD timeline)
🧠 Purpose / Goal

The goal of this dataset is to build a causal and interconnected understanding of human history, rather than treating events, ideas, and economic systems as isolated elements.

It is designed to:

Show how crises and historical events act as turning points in shaping economic systems and intellectual thought
Demonstrate how thinkers and scholars are influenced by their surrounding environments and historical pressures
Reveal the evolution of money as a response to societal and economic needs across civilizations
Highlight the interdependence between society, knowledge, and financial systems over time

Overall, the project aims to provide a multi-layered analytical framework that allows deeper thinking about how civilizations evolve, how ideas emerge, and how economic systems transform through history.
